Privacy Policy

Calculator 6 (Package: calculator6.in)

Last Updated: November 11, 2026
Summary: Calculator 6 respects your privacy. All calculations, financial computations, unit conversions, and history logs are processed 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Calculator 6 does not require account creation, sign-in, or registration. The application operates completely offline for core calculations and does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

2. Data Storage and Local Processing

All data entered into Calculator 6 remains on your local device:

  • Calculation History: Previous calculations are saved locally using an embedded SQLite/Room database on your device. You can search, copy, or delete individual entries, or clear all history at any time through the app settings.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as theme selection (Dark/Light/System), language preferences, haptic feedback, sound effects, decimal precision, and number formatting are stored locally in your device's private storage.

3. Device Permissions

Calculator 6 requires minimal system permissions solely to enhance user experience:

  • VIBRATE: Optional permission used for haptic tactile feedback when tapping calculator keys (can be disabled in Settings).

Calculator 6 does NOT request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo gallery, storage files, or phone state.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Calculator 6 does not use any third-party tracking tools, analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or data brokers. No data is sent to third-party servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Calculator 6 does not address anyone under the age of 13 intentionally. Since our application does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is fully safe for use by users of all ages, including children.

6. Security

We value your trust. Because all data resides locally on your Android device within the application's isolated sandbox, your data is protected by Android's built-in operating system security features.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy page.
